Life as a probabilistic problem.
Think of living life as a probabilistic problem you're trying to solve. Everyone has their own goal to achieve, and at any moment you have a sample space of actions to choose from. The actions you take need to be sequential, not episodic — each one building on the last and setting up what comes next, rather than independent episodes judged in isolation.
