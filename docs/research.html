Aug 2026 – Present   Undergraduate Researcher
Prof. Mukulika Maity, IIT Madras

Device-free human activity recognition using Channel State Information (CSI) from WiFi signals — CSI acquisition (Nexmon, PicoScenes), preprocessing (phase sanitization, denoising), and feature extraction (amplitude, phase, Doppler). Main focus: multi-person sensing with a single transmitter (Tx) and multiple receivers (Rx). Exploring CNN, LSTM, and Transformer-based models to classify activity from CSI features.
