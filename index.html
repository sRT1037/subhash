AI Knowledge

AI is something that can be used to accelerate any kind of desk work — and going forward, it's moving into 3D/physical work too (Boston Dynamics, and China, are making real progress there). At its core it's a neural network with multiple layers, structured loosely like a brain.

The thing Claude Code lacks compared to a brain is long-term memory — it tends to hallucinate/guess rather than recall. During my time at Rubrik I worked around this using a knowledge graph (via Obsidian) as long-term memory, combined with agents working in a structured way — a mini agentic framework.

AI is mostly three things: Compute, Algorithms, and Data. Compute is handled by NVIDIA/AMD; Algorithms by labs like Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind (and Chinese counterparts like Kimi/Moonshot, GLM); Data is the ongoing bottleneck everyone's racing to solve.

Also thinking about: US-China AI competition, open-source vs closed models, jailbreaking vs prompt injection, and what it means for a model to move from finding vulnerabilities to exploiting them.

Built an agentic workflow to speed up coding during my time at Rubrik.
